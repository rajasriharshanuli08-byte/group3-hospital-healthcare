# Group 3: Hospital & Healthcare Website (NovaCare)

A comprehensive, responsive healthcare and hospital portal with clinical departments, doctor specialist profiles, medical services, appointment booking, health articles, and emergency contacts.

## Assignment Overview
- **Category:** Group 3 — Project 4 (Hospital & Healthcare Website)
- **Objective:** Create a healthcare webpage with hospital information, departments, doctor profiles, medical services, appointment booking, health information, and emergency contacts.
- **Tech Stack:** HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flexbox, CSS Grid, Custom Cards), Vanilla JavaScript, FontAwesome Icons, Google Fonts.

## Features
1. **Emergency & Hotline Top Bar:** 24/7 Trauma line, ambulance dispatch, visiting hours, and instant booking button.
2. **Hero Section:** Clinical headline, JCI & NABH accreditation badge, consultation CTA, emergency phone callout, and satisfaction statistics.
3. **Quick Features Strip:** 24/7 Emergency Care, Advanced Diagnostics (3T MRI, Robotic Surgery), Clinical Labs, and In-House Pharmacy.
4. **Hospital Overview & About:** 28+ years of clinical excellence history, smart inpatient monitoring, and full insurance coverage.
5. **Medical Departments:** Dedicated service cards for Cardiology, Neurology, Pediatrics, Orthopedics, Oncology, and Ophthalmology.
6. **Doctor Specialist Profiles:** Clinical head cards featuring qualifications (Harvard, Johns Hopkins, Stanford), clinical experience, biographies, and direct appointment pre-fill triggers.
7. **Comprehensive Medical Services:** Intensive care suites (ICU/CCU), robotic surgery, encrypted telehealth consultations, and licensed blood bank.
8. **Interactive Appointment Booking Form:** Select department, doctor, preferred date, patient contact details, and symptoms with immediate booking verification.
9. **Health Information & Wellness Guides:** Clinically written wellness articles covering cardiovascular health, nutritional immunology, and cognitive burnout.
10. **Emergency Contacts & Location Finder:** Immediate ambulance dispatch hotlines, hospital campus directions, and administrative ombudsman contacts.
